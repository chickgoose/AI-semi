## 1:5:5:1 and base recommendation

Fovea's native contract applies row weights `[1,5,5,1]`, whose ideal shares are
`[1/12,5/12,5/12,1/12]`. Cluster2 provides two native lanes per row and thereby
flattens/transforms that weighted-service contract toward equal row opportunity.
The recovered archive has no independent continuous-all-16-source policy
artifact, so this is a structural contract distinction—not an archive-measured
policy PASS. Weight preservation remains HOLD until that artifact exists.

For the current scalar A7 link/interface base, recommend **Fovea**, specifically
because it presents one event at a time and retains the 1:5:5:1 service contract.
Cluster2 is the demonstrated **raw native capacity winner** and should be kept
for a future redesigned parallel link. No claim of absolute performance or PPA
superiority follows: the interfaces/retire widths differ, physical PPA is absent,
and the execution receipt is non-official.
