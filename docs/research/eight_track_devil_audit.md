### 2.2 Shared omissions and biases

The frozen 46 are a strong deterministic functional set, but not a universal
ranking set:

1. The manifest contains no `backpressure_shock` run.  Trace mode defaults to
   all retire lanes ready, even though the TB has separate built-in stall tests.
   Thus 46/46 says nothing about native sink-stall behavior.  A8 demonstrates
   the gap: its adapter discards `retire_ready` and still passes 46/46.
2. N=16 is the only frozen size.  A4/A7/A8/A9 physical-scaling hypotheses at
   N=32/64/256 are model or local structural extrapolations.
3. Offered load tops out at 2 events/cycle.  This reaches the K=2 ceiling but
   cannot establish K=4 utilization or a four-lane system win; at uniform 2.0,
   A7 K=4 is necessarily only about 50% utilized.
4. Events use fixed polarity/type and source-derived coordinate identity.  This
   is especially favorable to A6, whose v2 core does not transport arbitrary
   common payload and reconstructs a six-bit coordinate event from source ID.
5. The common source is a one-entry latch with at most one new occurrence per
   source/cycle.  Extra DUT storage can reduce source overrun simply by moving
   events across the acceptance boundary.  A2 and A4 must therefore be compared
   with equal-capacity controls, not only the small common mock.
6. Identity/affine/mirror controls are useful but do not span placement.  A4
   and A9 are topology-sensitive; broader mapping, wirelength, and congestion
   remain candidate-owned evidence, not frozen coverage.
7. Percentiles exclude source-overrun events.  Overrun, pending-at-right-edge,
   zero-service windows, and tails must remain side by side; otherwise buffering
   and codec selection can make delivered-event latency/compressibility look
   better by censoring offered traffic.

## 6. Cross-track ranking rules and final recommendation

The following claims must be prohibited in any combined presentation:

- no candidate may turn source-latch overrun into “lossless” delivery;
- no one-lane candidate may claim sustained bandwidth above one event/cycle;
- A7/A9 raw throughput must be accompanied by K, utilization, 22 pins/lane,
  and same-K/L reference;
- buffering wins must include candidate storage and compare equal capacity;
- codec results must use offered-stream entropy plus actual fixed-pin cycles,
  both endpoints, delimiters, and arbitrary-payload capability;
- zero-delay VCD/generic gates/depth are proxies, never power/Fmax/PPA;
- dirty/untracked results cannot satisfy a committed preregistered gate; and
- 46/46 always-ready correctness cannot be reported as backpressure support.

Recommended shortlist after this adversarial audit:

1. **A2 B4/D16: conditional one-lane keep**, pending a committed canonical
   equivalence check and its already-frozen equal-boundary local physical gate.
2. **A7 N=16/K=4: conditional four-lane keep**, only for a system boundary that
   independently justifies and charges 88 retire pins and four sinks.
3. **A3, A5, A6, A8, A9: reject/terminated** on existing functional or utility
   evidence.  **A4 N=16: reject**; N=64 remains research, not shortlist.

These two conditional keeps are not directly ranked against each other: A2 is
a one-lane buffering/bypass design and A7 is a four-lane compaction primitive.
Choosing between them before fixing the system lane/pin contract would recreate
the normalization error this audit is intended to prevent.  No unresolved
physical question above is evidence of a win; it is only the precise question
that a future common, endpoint-complete flow would need to answer.
