## Frozen address-grant replay

These numbers use exact ordered **addresses**, not the earlier analytical
row-bitmap interpretation.  Thus they supersede the second-round bitmap-model
number for this implemented boundary.

| Suite | generated | accepted | overrun | fixed-window throughput | mean / p99 / max latency |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| full50 | 106,416 | 93,645 | 12,771 | 0.806964 | 3.225 / 8 / 267 |
| capacity22 | 65,616 | 57,280 | 8,336 | 1.030404 | 3.328 / 9 / 267 |

The earlier receipt popped the firing pending occurrence before it admitted the
same indexed-cycle workload occurrence.  The pinned common TB does the reverse:
it offers at negedge, observes the old pending level, and only then accepts the
registered owner bundle at the following posedge.  The corrected old-to-new
deltas are:

| Suite | accepted | overrun | fixed-window delivered | throughput | mean / p99 / max latency |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| full50 | -5,890 | +5,890 | -5,912 | -0.050980 | +0.809 / 0 / -4 |
| capacity22 | -3,749 | +3,749 | -3,764 | -0.067756 | +0.768 / +1 / -4 |

The extra fixed-window reduction beyond the acceptance delta is intentional.
The replay analytically applies the common binding's specified one-cycle
owner-fire-to-retire latency, so a fire on the last stimulus service edge is
outside the fixed common measurement window. The 72-run lockstep itself covers
the owner RTL; separate common-binding directed tests execute the charged
adapter RTL and establish that latency and its transport tail.
The previous weighted-scalar/Cluster2 gain-retention comparison is superseded
because its A3 side used the old occurrence ordering. Persistent capacity is
still two addresses per committed bundle, with 240 address grants in 120
bundles; exact scalar-prefix policy RTL did not change.

The maximum latency of 267 is workload/policy evidence, not a universal bound.
The 5:1 policy plus sparse fallback and one-slot/source overrun semantics can
produce long finite-tail cases even though persistent all-row service is
periodic.
